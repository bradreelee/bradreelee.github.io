MyGo+ is a telematics app for safer driving, launched in February 2025 by Indonesian general insurer PT Lippo General Insurance (LGI). It scores driving from distance and acceleration data and turns the scores into reward points that can be redeemed for vouchers. Available on iOS and Android.
