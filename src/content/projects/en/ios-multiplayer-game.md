A cooperative adventure game built by a team of three in the iOS App Development 2020/21 course at RWTH Aachen, using SceneKit and MultipeerConnectivity.

- Tilt the device to move your character with the accelerometer, and tap the left or right half of the screen to grab obstacles or other players with that hand.
- Three modes: tutorial, single play, and team play, where you invite nearby players to play together.
- Successful grabs trigger haptic and sound feedback, and players can customize their character's body and hands.
